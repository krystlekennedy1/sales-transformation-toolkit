## All findings

### 1. Slow lead response: Web

46% of Web leads wait over 24 hours (typical 3%); converted 15% vs 32% when fast.

- Estimated impact: $679,920 (slow leads x conversion gap x avg deal x win rate)
- Confidence: 95% (sample of 2066)
- Owner: Marketing and sales operations, effort low
- First fix: Set a first-response target for this source and route new leads with an alert and an owner.

### 2. Unapproved discounts: Partner

91 deals over the 15% limit with no approval (26% of Partner deals, typical 0%).

- Estimated impact: $380,586 (discount given without approval)
- Confidence: 95% (sample of 353)
- Owner: Deal desk or finance, effort medium
- First fix: Block quote approval above the limit until an approver is recorded.

### 3. New sellers behind ramp: M12

3 new sellers behind ramp plan: low_activity, weak_pipeline.

- Estimated impact: $260,790 (planned bookings shortfall, next 90 days)
- Confidence: 95% (sample of 4)
- Owner: Enablement and the manager, effort medium
- First fix: Use the ramp coach plan for each flagged new seller and review weekly.

### 4. Repeated close date pushes: M02

open deals have been pushed 2.1 times on average (typical 0.4).

- Estimated impact: $169,067 (pipeline pushed 2+ times x win rate)
- Confidence: 95% (sample of 40)
- Owner: Frontline manager, effort medium
- First fix: Require a stated reason on every close date push and review repeat pushes in the weekly deal review.

### 5. Forecast bias: M05

forecast ran 59% above actual bookings in 5 of 5 months.

- Estimated impact: $207,588 (total forecast error in dollars)
- Confidence: 75% (sample of 5)
- Owner: Sales leader, effort medium
- First fix: Review forecast calls against outcomes with the manager and agree on commit criteria.

- Estimated impact: $20,640 (late-invoiced revenue x delay cost)

### 12. Incomplete handoffs: M12

71% of 17 won deals handed off with missing fields (typical 4%).

- Estimated impact: $1,350 (rework hours x hourly cost)
- Confidence: 78% (sample of 17)
- Owner: Sales operations, effort low
- First fix: Make the handoff fields required before a deal can move to closed won.

## Assumptions behind the dollar figures

- Open pipeline closes at 25%.
- A late-invoiced deal loses 5% of its value to delay and collection risk.
- Repairing one incomplete handoff takes 1.5 hours at $75 per hour.
- Forecast error is counted in full dollars, so it is not strictly a loss.

Impacts are in different units (lost bookings, rework cost, forecast error), so the ranking is a starting order for discussion, not a precise ordering.
